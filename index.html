Privacy Policy

Don’t Make Me Fall · last updated 21 August 2026

This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Android app com.skyseeker.game.

The game has no accounts, no login and no social features. It never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera, and it does not use GPS location.

What is collected

DataWhyWho processes it
A random install identifier, generated on your device the first time you play To count how many people play and how often, without identifying anyone. It is a random value created by the app, not a device or advertising ID, and it is deleted when you uninstall or clear the app’s data. PostHog
Gameplay events: runs started and ended, score, height reached, duration, which tower and which bird, whether a revive was offered or taken, tutorial progress To understand whether the game is too hard, too easy, or losing people at a particular point, and to balance it. PostHog
Technical context: app version, operating system, device model, game engine version, and a per-session identifier To tell crashes and problems on one kind of device from problems in the game itself. PostHog
Your device’s advertising ID and standard ad request data To show ads. In the game these are optional: an ad plays only if you choose to watch one to continue a run, or between runs. Google AdMob
Your consent choice To remember what you answered so you are not asked on every launch. Google (User Messaging Platform), stored on your device

Analytics events are sent without a person profile and with IP-based location lookup switched off, so they are not built into a profile of you and are not used to infer where you are.

Your game progress

Best scores, unlocked towers, your chosen bird and your settings are stored only on your own device. They are not uploaded anywhere and are lost if you uninstall the game.

Who the data is shared with

  • Google AdMob serves the ads and, if you consent, personalises them. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • PostHog receives the anonymous gameplay analytics described above. See the PostHog privacy policy.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Where the data goes

Analytics are processed on PostHog’s United States infrastructure, and Google processes ad data in the United States and elsewhere. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, that means your data may be transferred outside your country under the safeguards those providers publish in the policies linked above.

Advertising consent, and how to change your mind

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your advertising consent the first time you open it, using Google’s consent form. You can refuse. You can change your answer at any time from inside the game:

Settings (the gear on the main screen) → Controls → Privacy options.

That row appears only for players Google tells us have a choice to make, which is why you may not see it outside those regions.

To reset the advertising ID itself, use your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones, where you can also delete it entirely.

Children

The game is rated for everyone but is not directed at children, and it is not part of a families programme. It is declared to Google as a general-audience app and asks for ads rated G.

How long data is kept

Analytics events are retained for as long as they are useful for balancing the game and are deleted on the providers’ standard retention schedules. Your consent choice and your game progress live on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Because the analytics are tied only to a random identifier created on your device and never to your name or account, the fastest and most complete way to exercise deletion is to clear the app’s data or uninstall it, which severs the link permanently. If you want a request handled directly, contact the address below and include the install identifier if you still have it.

Changes to this policy

If what the game collects changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
